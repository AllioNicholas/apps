Frequently asked questions

A plate wasn't recognized

Fill the on-screen frame with the plate, use the zoom for plates further away, and avoid strong glare. You can always tap Type Plate and enter it by hand, or fix misread characters on the result screen before adding it.

The camera doesn't work

Taferl needs camera access to snap plates. Turn it on in Settings › Privacy & Security › Camera › Taferl. Without it you can still pick a photo or type a plate.

Why can I pick between different meanings for a code?

Some codes are used for more than one thing. “K”, for example, is both Klagenfurt and the Kärnten state government. Look at the crest on the plate to choose the right one.

My collection doesn't appear on my other devices

Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on for Taferl on each device. Syncing can take a few minutes.

How do I delete a sighting?

Open the Logbook tab and swipe left on the sighting.
