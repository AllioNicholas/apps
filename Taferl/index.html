Taferl

Spot them all: collect every Austrian license plate code.

Coming soon to the App Store

Snap a license plate, pick a photo or type it in. Taferl tells you which district, authority or embassy it belongs to and adds it to your collection.

5 collections

  • Districts & CitiesEvery active district and statutory city code.
  • Federal & OfficialPolice, army, post and other public authorities.
  • Diplomatic & ConsularEmbassies and consulates.
  • Service PlatesReserved endings like TX for taxis.
  • Retired CodesNo longer issued, but still on the road.

Points, ranks and awards

Every new code earns points, and rarer plates are worth more. A district is worth 10 points, a diplomatic plate 40 and a retired code 50. Climb from Learner to Autobahn Legend, and unlock awards like Grand Tour (all nine Bundesländer), Capital Collector and Every Corner of Austria.

Smart recognition

  • Camera: fit the plate in the frame and snap. Zoom in on plates further away, in portrait or landscape.
  • Photos: pick a picture you took earlier.
  • Typing: enter the plate as you saw it, for example “W 12345 X”.
  • Two-line motorcycle plates work too, and when a code means more than one thing (like “K” for Klagenfurt or the Kärnten government) Taferl lets you choose.

Your logbook

Every sighting is saved with its photo and date, so you can look back on where you spotted each plate. Your collection syncs across your devices with iCloud.

Privacy

Taferl doesn't collect any personal data. Photos are recognized on your device, and your collection is stored on your device and in your private iCloud account. The developer can't see it.
