SpeedyEye

Challenge your friends and discover who's the quickest finger!

Download on the App Store

Tap the numbers 1 to 25 on a 5×5 board as fast as you can. Simple to learn, hard to master.

3 game modes

  • Up CountSelect numbers in ascending order.
  • Down CountSelect numbers in descending order.
  • RandomSelect numbers in the random order shown by the helper label.

Game Center

Track your best scores on the leaderboards and keep an eye on your opponents.

Start a game from anywhere

  • Home Screen quick actions: touch and hold the app icon.
  • Siri: say “Play Up Count in SpeedyEye” or “Start a SpeedyEye game in Random”.
  • Spotlight and Shortcuts: search for SpeedyEye or add the Start Game action to your shortcuts, the Action Button or Control Center.

Built for everyone

  • VoiceOver reads every control, the countdown and the next number to find.
  • Text scales with Dynamic Type, up to the largest accessibility sizes.
  • Animations calm down when Reduce Motion is on.
  • Available in English and Italian.

Privacy

SpeedyEye doesn't collect any personal data. Your best times stay on your device, and Game Center scores are handled by Apple.
